# Pilot agreement: template

> **Not legal advice.** This is a plain-language starting point for a small pilot. Have a lawyer or CA review it before client 3, and check GST treatment with a CA.

---

**Lead Response Pilot Agreement**

**Between:** Thrumline ("we"), [address, contact], and [Client business name] ("you"), [address, contact person].
**Start date:** [date] **Pilot ends:** [start + 30 days]

### 1. What we will do
- Reply on WhatsApp to new leads from: [list sources, e.g. Meta lead form "Project X", website form] within 60 seconds, 24×7.
- Ask your approved qualifying questions, offer your approved visit or appointment slots, and alert [salesperson name(s)] on WhatsApp when a lead is ready.
- Follow up with leads who don't reply on day 1, 3 and 7 using templates you approve.
- Send a weekly report every Monday covering leads, reply time, qualified leads, bookings, handoffs and opt-outs.

**Not included:** phone calls, ad management, CRM integrations other than a shared Google Sheet, and anything not listed above unless agreed in writing.

### 2. Success metric
Agreed metric: [e.g. booked visits per 100 leads]. Baseline: [value, from which period and source]. Target: [value].
At the end of the pilot we review the metric together. If it has not improved, you can stop and owe nothing further.

### 3. Fees
- Pilot: **₹9,999**, including setup, paid **before** work starts.
- If you continue: **₹7,999 per month**, paid in advance. Cancel any time with 7 days' notice before the next billing date.
- WhatsApp message charges are billed by Meta **directly to your account** and are not part of our fee.
- Prices exclude GST if applicable.
- **We do not guarantee any number of leads, bookings or sales.**

### 4. Your responsibilities
- Provide access to your Meta Business Manager, WhatsApp Business account and lead sources.
- Approve in writing the facts sheet (prices, projects or services, timings), templates and qualifying questions before go-live, and tell us about any changes.
- Make sure your lead forms include consent to be contacted on WhatsApp.
- Have someone respond to hot-lead alerts.

### 5. The AI assistant
The assistant answers only from the facts you approve and hands off to your team when it is unsure. It can still make mistakes. We review conversations weekly and fix issues promptly. The assistant will not offer discounts or make commitments beyond what you approve.

### 6. Your customers' data
- You own your leads' data. We process it **only** to provide this service and on your instructions.
- We collect only what the service needs (name, phone, answers to qualifying questions, conversation history). We keep it access-controlled and never share or sell it.
- We will tell you within 24 hours of discovering any breach affecting your data.
- When the agreement ends, we give you an export on request and delete your data within 30 days.
- Your customers can opt out at any time. Opt-outs are honoured immediately.

### 7. Liability
Each party's total liability under this agreement is limited to the fees you paid us in the 3 months before the claim. Neither party is liable for lost profits.

### 8. Ending the agreement
Either party can end it with 7 days' written notice (WhatsApp or email is fine). The data terms in section 6 continue until deletion is complete.
