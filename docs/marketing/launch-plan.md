# Marketing launch plan (zero budget, runs alongside the validation sprint)

**Status:** plan only. Nothing is set up yet.
**Budget:** ₹0 in ads. Domain and email cost roughly ₹1,000–1,500 a year (estimate; check Hostinger's current prices).
**Ready-to-post copy:** `docs/marketing/content.md`. **Brand rules:** `brand/BRAND.md`.

## The honest part first

- **Content will not get you your first 3 clients.** The response-time audit, walk-ins and warm introductions will. Content exists so that when an owner looks you up after your WhatsApp message, you look real.
- **No paid ads until 3 pilots have paid** (D-003). Before that, ad money buys attention for an offer that hasn't been proven.
- **Don't contaminate your own data.** Don't post anything about the response-time study until every enquiry has been sent. After that, publish **anonymised totals only**, and only once N ≥ 30.
- **Your own speed is the product demo.** Every channel below points people to your WhatsApp. If a prospect waits 3 hours for your reply, the pitch is dead.

## Weekly metrics

Track these in a sheet next to `pipeline.csv`:
- Conversations started by channel (ask everyone "how did you hear about Thrumline?")
- Your own reply time to inbound WhatsApp messages (median). Hold yourself to the standard you sell.
- Conversations → pilots proposed → pilots paid

## Never

- Paid ads before 3 paid pilots.
- Bulk WhatsApp broadcasts to bought or scraped lists. They get your number banned, and unsolicited commercial messages can breach TRAI rules.
- Fake reviews, testimonials, client logos or follower counts.
- Naming or screenshotting any business from the audits.
- Promising sales or revenue results.
