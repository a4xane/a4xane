Why this matters

A Harvard Business Review study of 2,241 US companies found that firms that tried to contact a web lead within an hour were nearly 7× as likely to qualify it as firms that waited even an hour longer, and more than 60× as likely as firms that waited 24 hours or more.

Source: Oldroyd, McElheran & Elkington, “The Short Life of Online Sales Leads”, HBR, March 2011. That study was done in the US in 2011, so treat it as a direction, not a forecast for your business.
