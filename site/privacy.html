Privacy Policy

Effective 24 September 2026

Thrumline ("we") is run by Kanish Shah from Vapi, Gujarat, India. This page explains what personal data we handle, why, and what you can ask us to do with it.

Contact for any privacy question or request: Kanish Shah · WhatsApp or call +91 83470 55841

1. This website

This website does not use cookies, analytics or advertising trackers, and its fonts are hosted on this site. Our hosting provider may keep standard server logs (such as IP address, browser type and time of request) for security and reliability.

2. When you contact us

If you message or call us, we receive your name, phone number and whatever you send us. We use it only to reply to you and, if you become a client, to provide and bill our services. We keep it for as long as we are in conversation or working together, and delete it when you ask, unless we have to keep something (such as an invoice) by law. WhatsApp is operated by Meta, and Meta's own privacy policy applies to how WhatsApp handles your messages.

3. Our clients' customers

When a business uses Thrumline, we reply to the people who enquire with that business (their "leads") on the business's behalf. For that data, the business decides what it is used for, and we act only on its instructions.

  • What we process: name, phone number, the answers the person gives to the business's questions, booking details and the conversation.
  • Why: to reply to the enquiry, ask the business's qualifying questions, book a visit or appointment, alert the business's team and report results to the business.
  • Who else is involved: Meta (WhatsApp Business Platform), an AI model provider that drafts replies using only information the business has approved, and the hosting provider for our workflow software. They process the data only to provide the service.
  • What we don't do: we don't sell personal data, share it with other clients, or use one client's data for another client.
  • Opting out: anyone who replies "STOP" or asks not to be contacted is not messaged again.
  • How long: while the business is our client. We delete the business's data within 30 days after it stops using Thrumline, after giving it an export if it asks.

If you enquired with one of our clients and want to access, correct or delete your data, you can ask that business or ask us, and we will help.

4. Security

Access to personal data is limited to the people who need it to run the service. Credentials are stored securely, and each client's data and accounts are kept separate. If a breach affects a client's data, we tell that client promptly so it can meet its own obligations.

5. Your rights

Under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, you can ask for a summary of the personal data we hold about you, ask us to correct or erase it, withdraw consent where we rely on it, and raise a grievance with us. Contact us using the details above. If you are not satisfied with our response, you can approach the Data Protection Board of India.

6. Changes

If we change this policy, we will update this page and the effective date above.
