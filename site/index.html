The leak

You paid for the lead. Then it waited.

Ads, portals and listings cost money. What happens in the first few minutes after someone enquires decides whether that money turns into a visit, or into a sale for someone else.

13 h

The overnight gap

An enquiry at 10 PM that gets a reply at 11 AM leaves the buyer 13 hours to call someone else, and your competitor's number is one tap away.

"We'll get back"

The auto-greeting trap

An automatic "thanks, we'll contact you soon" feels like a reply to you. To the buyer it's a wait, with no answer and no next step.

1 message

No follow-up

A lead who doesn't answer the first message gets forgotten. Nobody has time to chase every enquiry three times.

7×

A Harvard Business Review study of 2,241 US companies found that firms that tried to contact a web lead within an hour were nearly 7× as likely to qualify it as firms that waited even an hour longer, and more than 60× as likely as firms that waited 24 hours or more.

Oldroyd, McElheran & Elkington, "The Short Life of Online Sales Leads", HBR, March 2011. A US study from 2011: treat it as a direction, not a forecast for your business.

Questions

Straight answers.

Is this a chatbot?

It's an assistant that only knows what you approve: your projects or services, prices, timings, location and FAQs. It asks your qualifying questions and books visits. When it doesn't know something, it says so and hands over to your team. It won't make up answers or chat about unrelated topics.

What if it says something wrong?

It answers only from your approved facts sheet, never offers discounts or makes commitments you haven't approved, and hands over when it's unsure. You approve every message template before go-live, and we review a sample of real conversations every week and fix anything off.

Is this allowed on WhatsApp?

Yes, for this kind of assistant. Since 15 January 2026, Meta's WhatsApp Business rules ban general-purpose AI chatbots, but allow assistants that handle a business's own customer service, bookings and sales, which is exactly what this is. We also keep to WhatsApp's opt-in rules: your lead forms say people may be contacted on WhatsApp, and anyone who says stop is never messaged again.

Do I need a new phone number?

You need a number connected to the WhatsApp Business Platform. If your current number is on the WhatsApp Business app, we'll go through the options with you (moving it, or using a new number) before anything changes.

Who owns the data?

You do. The system runs on your own WhatsApp Business account, leads are logged to a sheet you can see, and we only use the data to run your system. If you stop, we give you an export on request and delete your data within 30 days.

What will WhatsApp charge me?

Meta charges per message for certain message types and bills your account directly. The amount depends on your lead volume, and we estimate it for your numbers before you start.

Which languages?

English, Hindi and Gujarati text. Before going live, we test with real examples of how your leads write.

I already use Privyr, AiSensy or a CRM.

If it works and your team replies fast, keep it. We'll tell you if you don't need us. In the pilot we log leads to a Google Sheet; if you use a CRM, we'll tell you honestly whether we can connect it.

Is there a lock-in?
